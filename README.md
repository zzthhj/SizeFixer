## 🇺🇸 English

### 1. Purpose

The app **SizeFixer** is designed only to help you prepare photos for upload: compress file size, convert formats, and resize dimensions. It does not perform any other functions.

### 2. Data Privacy

SizeFixer runs entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, upload, or share your personal data or photos.

### 3. Photos & Files

Access is used only to:

- select photos from your library or import image files;
- save processed results to Files or Photos when you choose.

All processing is done locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to our servers.

### 4. Exported Files

When you export, SizeFixer may remove hidden metadata, such as GPS and camera EXIF, from output files. Only the image data you choose to save is written to your device.

### 5. In-App Purchases

Optional save credits and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card or Apple ID details.
